How the cycles were run

In brief

Each cycle asked one question, used the cheapest method that could answer it, and set its pass or fail rule before the result was known. A failed check ended or redirected that line of work.

  • Cheap checks first. Quick geometry and positive-control checks were run before any expensive simulation, and a simulation was run only on what survived the check before it.
  • Rules set beforehand. Thresholds and file hashes were recorded before each Boltz-2 batch was launched. The thresholds are provisional until the team confirms them; any later change must be reported as made after the fact.
  • Failures kept. Invalid analyses were renamed, not deleted, and the reason is given in the register below.
  • Same-study comparison only. Force-field energies and cavity volumes are used to compare runs within this study; they are not binding free energies.

Structure prediction: five Boltz-2 rounds

In brief

Five rounds of Boltz-2 runs, 25 runs and 125 models in rounds 2 to 5. Each began from the previous result. The main outcome was a negative one: the variation between seeds was as large as the effect of the substitutions.

Round 1

Takeaway: The pose looked constraint-dependent.

  • Design. Does the ketoacyl synthase (KS) pocket hold a C14 chain, and which residues are off limits?
  • Build. C14 in the KS domain, unconstrained and with distance caps (6 and 8 Å); C16 and C22 as controls. Google Colab, one sample per run.
  • Test. Unconstrained ligand ipTM 0.511; constrained 0.80 to 0.83. An overlap with M1251 of 0.96 to 1.77 Å was reported.
  • Learn. The pose looked constraint-dependent, so the next round re-ran the setup on laboratory GPUs and tested the reported overlap.

Round 2

Takeaway: The reported M1251 overlap did not recur.

  • Design. Does the result depend on how the run is set up?
  • Build. 7 runs, 35 models on RTX A6000 GPUs: unconstrained baseline, cutoffs of 6, 8 and 10 Å, V1254W, C16 and C22; seed 42, soft contact on C1305.
  • Test. Baseline ligand 3.0 to 4.6 Å from C1305 (ipTM 0.80). M1251 no closer than 3.3 Å. M1260 at 15 to 17 Å.
  • Learn. The M1251 overlap did not recur. The pose seemed to follow the constrained residue, so the gate residues were constrained directly.

Round 3

Takeaway: Confidence did not separate the setups.

  • Design. Does the pose follow the residues it is told to touch?
  • Build. 8 runs, 40 models: forced contacts on M1251 and M1260 (one run also V1254), with steering potentials on or off.
  • Test. M1260 at 3.9 to 6.5 Å. No overlap with M1251 with or without potentials. Confidence 0.90 to 0.94 in every run.
  • Learn. Confidence did not separate the setups, so controls (a repeat, a pocket-plug mutant, a decoy ligand) were added.

Round 4

Takeaway: A same-seed repeat is not a replicate.

  • Design. Does the model respond to a change in the pocket, and does a rerun agree?
  • Build. 4 runs, 20 models in a 30-minute window: wild-type repeat, a four-tryptophan “plug” mutant, a caffeine decoy, V1254W.
  • Test. Same-seed repeat identical (0.00 Å). Plug moved the ligand 1.25 Å (ipTM 0.870 vs 0.861). V1254 distance 5.88 vs 6.33 Å.
  • Learn. A repeat with the same seed proves nothing about variation, so a second seed and more substitutions were run.

Round 5

Takeaway: Seed variation matched the substitution effects.

  • Design. How large is the variation between seeds, and what do further substitutions and ligands do?
  • Build. 6 runs, 30 models: wild type with seed 43, M1251W, V1254A, C22, C8 and a glucose decoy.
  • Test. Seed 43 vs 42: V1254 median +0.82 Å, ligand RMSD median 2.14 Å. C22 reached M1260 (6.9 Å vs 15.5 Å for C14).
  • Learn. Seed variation was as large as the substitution effects, so distances are read as descriptive and independent methods were added (cycles 0 to 5 below).

Independent checks: docking, cavity and force-field relaxation

In brief

Six further cycles checked the Boltz-2 poses with methods that do not share its weaknesses. Docking failed its own control. Relaxation and cavity checks agreed with Boltz-2 on one point: only the four-residue plug is clearly outside the noise.

Cycle 0

Takeaway: A second environment was needed (CUDA 12.8).

  • Design. Build one environment for docking, cavity and force-field work under the server’s data policy.
  • Build and test. Environment built. Two failures: the GPU runtime did not match the driver, and a package install upgraded numpy and left unrelated sub-packages with unmet requirements.
  • Learn. Computation moved to a second, separate environment pinned to CUDA 12.8.

Cycle 1

Takeaway: The copies are not independent; the noise floor comes from method sensitivity.

  • Design. Measure the six ketoacyl synthase copies in the 1.9 Å structure 8PRW, to get a noise floor for pocket geometry.
  • Build and test. The six copies are essentially identical (C1305 to M1251 8.5 to 8.8 Å; the M1251 chi1 angle −68° in all six). A cavity of about 175 to 215 Å³ sits at C1305, and its volume moves by about ±20 % with grid and probe settings.
  • Learn. The copies are not an independent estimate of variability, so the noise floor comes from method sensitivity. The detected cavity is far smaller than a C14 chain.

Cycle 2

Takeaway: Docking failed its control and was dropped.

  • Design. Can Vina docking recover the known decanoate pose in E. coli 1F91 (positive control)?
  • Build and test. Failed. Top-ranked poses were 4.8 to 10.1 Å from the crystal pose over 18 runs (rule: 3 Å). Scores differed by about 0.3 kcal/mol across the top five poses.
  • Learn. Docking is not used to compare poses or rank ligands. Poses are judged by force-field relaxation instead.

Cycle 3

Takeaway: Tooling was fixed and the retention rule set beforehand.

  • Design. Relax Boltz-2 poses by minimisation and short restrained simulation.
  • Build and test. Slow on the CPU, then blocked by the GPU runtime and ligand-parameter errors (see the register). A one-model test then took about 40 s.
  • Learn. Tooling was fixed; the pose-retention rule was set before any pose was relaxed.

Cycle 4

Takeaway: Only the four-residue plug lies outside the noise.

  • Design. Relax all 50 Boltz-2 models (wild type with seeds 42 and 43, plug, V1254W, V1254A, M1251W, C22, C8, caffeine, glucose).
  • Build and test. 50 of 50 models stayed in the pocket (ligand RMSD median 0.6 to 1.0 Å). Interaction energy (kJ/mol, median of five): wild type −115 and −108 for the two seeds, V1254W −129, V1254A −126, M1251W −125, plug −150, C22 −142, C8 −71, caffeine −90, glucose −59.
  • Learn. The retention check could not fail, so it shows only that poses are not collapsing artefacts. Only the plug lies clearly outside the wild-type spread; single substitutions differ by about the size of the seed difference.

Cycle 5

Takeaway: A V1254W tunnel effect cannot be tested on the apo structure.

  • Design. Compare the cavity at C1305 in wild type and built mutants on two 8PRW copies.
  • Build and test. Single substitutions changed the cavity by less than two identical wild-type copies differ from each other (139 vs 120 Å³ at default settings). The four-residue plug shrank it (74 and 107 Å³).
  • Learn. The cavity from C1305 never reaches V1254, so a tunnel-blocking effect of V1254W cannot be tested on the apo structure.

Provisional thresholds: a pose counts as retained if the minimised ligand RMSD is below 2.5 Å and the nearest ligand atom to C1305 SG stays within 2 Å of its starting value. Force-field settings: Amber14 protein, GAFF-2.11 ligand with MMFF94 partial charges, GBn2 implicit solvent, 200 ps with the backbone restrained. These energies rank runs within this study only.

Outcome and limits

In brief

The modelling did not confirm or refute V1254W. Docking failed its control, the Boltz-2 poses could not be told apart from seed noise except for the four-residue plug, and the longer simulations, extra seeds and a second model were not run.

What the cycles established [computed]

  • The reported overlap with M1251 was not reproduced on the laboratory GPUs, so it is not used as evidence.
  • Seed variation (ligand RMSD median 2.14 Å) was as large as the effect of single substitutions, so no conclusion about V1254W, V1254A or M1251W is drawn.
  • The four-tryptophan plug is the only variant clearly outside the wild-type spread, in interaction energy (−150 against −115 and −108 kJ/mol) and in cavity volume (74 and 107 against 139 and 120 Å3).
  • The decoys bound more weakly than C14 in the relaxed models (caffeine −90, glucose −59 kJ/mol), which is the expected direction but rests on one seed each.
  • Docking failed its positive control and was not used to rank anything.

What was not done or cannot be concluded

  • No result has been validated against wet-lab data. The engineered V1254W strain, and whether it works in baker’s yeast, remain a hypothesis. [hypothesis]
  • The 200 ps relaxations had a restrained backbone and all 50 poses passed, so they cannot show that a pose is stable. Unrestrained multi-nanosecond simulation is not done. [pending]
  • Wild type has two seeds and every variant one, so there is no variance estimate. At least five seeds per variant are not run. [pending]
  • No second co-folding model and no positive control for the whole pipeline were run. [pending]
  • A tunnel-blocking effect of V1254W cannot be tested on the apo structure, because its cavity never reaches V1254.
  • No strain proposals were issued, because the scoring function failed its own checks (see Trust).

Lessons learned

In brief

Run the control before trusting the method, estimate noise before reading effects, and test a check on a case that can fail. These changed what the later cycles did.

  • Run the positive control first. Docking was tried on a known complex before it was used on ours. It failed, so it was dropped before it could rank anything.
  • A repeat is not a replicate. The same-seed repeat was identical and said nothing about variation. A second seed showed that noise was as large as the substitution effects, and this changed how every earlier distance was read.
  • Confidence is not evidence. Boltz-2 confidence stayed at 0.90 to 0.94 in every setup, so it could not separate them. Controls and independent methods were needed.
  • A check must be able to fail. The retention rule was set before the relaxations were run, but all 50 poses passed. A threshold set in advance is not enough if the test cannot reject; the next version needs unrestrained simulation.
  • Test the analysis on a known case. Bond-length jumps of 2.5 and 5.2 Å exposed that the docking output reorders atoms, and a missing RMSD exposed a mis-read atom type. Both result sets were renamed, not deleted, and not used.
  • Do not rely on one sample per run. The first runs used one sample each and reported an overlap that later runs, with five samples, did not reproduce.
  • Test the tooling on one case before a batch. A one-model run (about 40 s) found the GPU-runtime and ligand-parameter errors before 50 models were queued. A separate environment pinned to the driver’s CUDA version avoided a second round of conflicts.
  • Record failures with causes. Causes marked ‘possibly’ in the register are untested and say so.

What we would do differently. Run a second seed in the first Boltz-2 round, and set up the pipeline positive control and unrestrained simulation at the start rather than after the fifth round.

Next cycles

In brief

Three cycles follow from what was learned: unrestrained simulation, more seeds, and a second co-folding model.

  • Unrestrained simulation. Replicated runs of 5 to 10 ns, starting from several Boltz-2 poses of wild type and V1254W, to test whether the ligand stays and whether V1254W changes the V1254-to-ligand distance beyond replicate spread. [pending]
  • More seeds. At least five seeds per variant, compared as distributions over all models rather than medians. [pending]
  • A second co-folding model as an independent check on Boltz-2, once licence and memory are confirmed. [pending]
  • Positive control for the pipeline. A structure with a known bound fatty acid, with a locally generated alignment. [pending]

The earlier strain-design cycle (a single mutation versus gene deletions, and the strategy that the model indicated should be abandoned) is on the Engineering page.
