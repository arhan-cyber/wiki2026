How the cycles were run

In brief

Each cycle asked one question, used the cheapest method that could answer it, and set its pass or fail rule before the result was known. A failed check ended or redirected that line of work.

  • Cheap checks first. Quick geometry and positive-control checks were run before any expensive simulation, and a simulation was run only on what survived the check before it.
  • Rules set beforehand. Thresholds and file hashes were recorded before each Boltz-2 batch was launched. The thresholds are provisional until the team confirms them; any later change must be reported as made after the fact.
  • Failures kept. Invalid analyses were renamed, not deleted, and the reason is given in the register below.
  • Same-study comparison only. Force-field energies and cavity volumes are used to compare runs within this study; they are not binding free energies.

Structure prediction: five Boltz-2 rounds

In brief

Five rounds of Boltz-2 runs, 25 runs and 125 models in rounds 2 to 5. Each began from the previous result. The main outcome was a negative one: the variation between seeds was as large as the effect of the substitutions.

Independent checks: docking, cavity and force-field relaxation

In brief

Six further cycles checked the Boltz-2 poses with methods that do not share its weaknesses. Docking failed its own control. Relaxation and cavity checks agreed with Boltz-2 on one point: only the four-residue plug is clearly outside the noise.

Next cycles

In brief

Three cycles follow from what was learned: unrestrained simulation, more seeds, and a second co-folding model.

  • Unrestrained simulation. Replicated runs of 5 to 10 ns, starting from several Boltz-2 poses of wild type and V1254W, to test whether the ligand stays and whether V1254W changes the V1254-to-ligand distance beyond replicate spread. [pending]
  • More seeds. At least five seeds per variant, compared as distributions over all models rather than medians. [pending]
  • A second co-folding model as an independent check on Boltz-2, once licence and memory are confirmed. [pending]
  • Positive control for the pipeline. A structure with a known bound fatty acid, with a locally generated alignment. [pending]

The earlier strain-design cycle (a single mutation versus gene deletions, and the strategy that the model indicated should be abandoned) is on the Engineering page.
