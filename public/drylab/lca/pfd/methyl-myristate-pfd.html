Methyl myristate process: energy & carbon map

Hover over (or tap) any unit in the flow diagram to see its electricity, heating and cooling demand and its share of the carbon footprint. All figures are for one batch (100 m³ fermenter), which yields 45.6 kg of 98.5 % methyl myristate.

Data sources & method
  • Energy demand (electricity, heating, cooling) per unit comes from the process design calculations (PFD calculations, Table 15). Tagged PDF.
  • Environmental impacts come from the openLCA model “MM – in-vivo methylation f = 0.2” (ecoinvent cut-off, EF v3.1, India, reference flow 45.6 kg methyl myristate). Tagged LCA. Where the two sources disagree, the LCA value is shown.
  • Carbon footprint per unit is an attribution of the LCA total: the LCA result is split into electricity supply, heat supply, fermentation feedstocks, solvents/catalyst, lime and other background processes. Electricity and heat impacts are shared out in proportion to each unit’s electricity and heating demand; feedstock impacts are assigned to BC-1, solvent and catalyst impacts to HR-1, lime to DCT-1, and the remainder in proportion to the rest. Unit values therefore add up exactly to the LCA total.
  • Cooling duties are met with cooling water, which the LCA counts as water use rather than greenhouse-gas emissions, so pure coolers and condensers carry no carbon footprint of their own.
  • Biogenic CO₂ from fermentation (3,679 kg) is reported but has a characterisation factor of zero in EF v3.1, because the carbon was recently taken up by the feedstock crops.
